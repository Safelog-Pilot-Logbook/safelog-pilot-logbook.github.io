FAQ

Is Safelog compliant with my authority?

Yes. Fully supports FAA (61.65, Parts 91/135/121), EASA/JAR-FCL, UK CAA CAP 407, Transport Canada, CASA, and all known global standards.

Do I need other devices?

No. The Mac app stands alone, but syncs effortlessly with phone, tablet, or web if you choose.

How do I import old logs?

Supports totals from paper logs, third-party eLogs, and CSV. Start fresh without re-entering history.

What’s new in 8.2.6?

More airline imports, derived fields for multi-format output, updated databases, UI improvements, and minor fixes.
